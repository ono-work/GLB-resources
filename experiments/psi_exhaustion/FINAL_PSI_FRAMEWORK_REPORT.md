## 6. Future routes (after exhaustion)

1. **P116 on a bigger machine.** The cell `P = [1, 6, 16]` lies outside Ψ (not anchored at 0); it was excluded by instruction.
2. **New P-worlds.** Anchors other than 0 / `*` (cells with `0 ∉ R`, `* ∉ R`), each needing its own key universe and separation argument.
3. **New constructions.** Families not of the form `R = T ∪ B` with a B-independent pool.
4. **Coupling.** Count `L ⊄ H_L(T ∪ C)` jointly with B (CELL_SCREENING finding 1).
5. **Thickening.** Bounds that do not enumerate exactly: injections or lower estimates of large sub-families, trading exactness for scale.
