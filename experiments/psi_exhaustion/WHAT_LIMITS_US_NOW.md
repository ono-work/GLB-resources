## B. The machine limit (not reached)

- Largest measured peak RSS: producer 21.17 GB (R_0_s), verifier 30.70 GB (R_0_0). The machine has 48 GB.
- Largest projected peak: 32.6 GiB. All producer and verifier logs (`/usr/bin/time -l`) report 0 swaps.
- Total wall time: producers 45.6 h, independent verifiers 62.7 h (sequential, one job at a time per queue).
- **No key of the framework was limited by RAM.** Every key is CERTIFIED; no key is INFEASIBLE.

## C. The construction limit (binding)

- All 36 keys of Ψ(S, C) are resolved (v5: 2, earlier ledger: 12, this campaign: 22). The framework total is B_Ψ = 2.9247e+40.
- The 22 new keys add 2.468e+39 (×2 included), i.e. ×1.0922 over B0 = 2.6779e+40, or 0.0383 decimal orders.
- The mass is concentrated: the top 5 keys give 93.1% of the new mass; after the 5th key each further key adds at most 2.40e+37.
- There is nothing left inside the framework to compute. More mass requires pairs outside Ψ, i.e. a new construction (see `PSI_FRAMEWORK_EXHAUSTIVENESS.md` §4):
  1. 0-world pairs with `L ⊄ H_L(T ∪ C)` (the pool depends on B: coupled L/B counting);
  2. cells not anchored at 0 (e.g. the P116 cell `P = [1, 6, 16]`), excluded in this round by instruction;
  3. Z-world pairs with `* ∉ R` (need a new mirror-separation argument).

**Conclusion: the limit now is the construction, not RAM.**
